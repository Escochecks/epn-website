Product overview

Escochex Payroll is organized around the work an administrator does. This page lists what you can do today.

Get started

Create your account, sign in, and move between companies.

  • Company sign-upRegister your company and Owner account in four guided steps.
  • Sign in and company selectionSign in with your email and password and choose which company to work in.
  • Switch between companiesMove between the companies you belong to without signing out.
  • Change your passwordChange your password and sign out of every other device.

Set up your business

Manage the companies on your account.

  • See your companiesSee the companies you belong to.

Manage your people

Employees, contractors, documents, and payroll readiness.

  • Employee listSearch and filter the All Employees list, see each employee's job role, export it to PDF, Excel or CSV, and terminate or reactivate employees.
  • Add an employee and edit personal informationCreate an employee, choosing their job role from your company's roles, and edit their name and email, with leave-plan setup.
  • Payroll readinessSee at a glance which employees have setup items blocking payroll, and what each one needs.
  • Employee documentsUpload, view, and archive documents such as W-4, I-9 and paystubs on an employee, and ask an employee to correct a file they uploaded.
  • ContractorsAdd contractors, find them, open a profile, and edit their details.

Time and time off

Daily hours, leave requests, balances, and holidays.

  • Time and attendanceEnter daily worked and overtime hours, then approve or reject them.
  • Time offSubmit and review leave requests, see balances, and manage company holidays.

Control access

Roles, permissions, and a record of activity.

  • Users, roles, and permissionsSee company members, change their role, remove their access, and read what each role can do.
  • Audit HistoryReview who did what across your company.

Your plan

See your plan and manage the billing contact.

  • Subscription and plansSee your plan (Core, Plus, or Pro Plus), compare plans, and manage the billing contact.

Still to come

Payroll processing and payroll reports are planned for a future release. See what to expect →

Payroll

Escochex Payroll is built to make payroll clear and carefully controlled. Start by getting your business, your people, and your access in order.

Get ready for payroll

These are available today.

  1. Prepare your business

    Register your company and Owner account, and choose your pay schedule during sign-up.

    Get started →

  2. Add your people

    Create employees and contractors and keep their documents together.

    Manage employees →

  3. Track payroll readiness

    See which employees have setup items to finish, and what each one needs.

    Check payroll readiness →

  4. Handle time and time off

    Enter daily hours, review leave requests, and keep holidays in one place.

    Time and attendance →

  5. Set permissions and stay secure

    Give teammates the right role and review activity in Audit History.

    Users and permissions →

Live payroll processing is planned for a future release

Until then, Escochex Payroll can’t run live payroll, send direct deposits, deposit taxes, or file tax forms. Everything above helps you be ready when it arrives.

Built for control from day one

Payroll data is sensitive. These protections are in the product today.

  • Role-based accessRoles decide who can view or change what.
  • Company-separated dataYou see only the company you select.
  • Audit HistoryActivity is recorded and reviewable.
  • Masked numbersSensitive numbers show only last digits.
  • Sign-in protectionAccounts lock after repeated failures.

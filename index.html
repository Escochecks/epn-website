What you can do today

Each capability links to a step-by-step guide.

  • Company sign-up

    Register your company and Owner account in four guided steps.

    Sign-up creates your company and Owner login. It does not start payroll or move money.

    Learn how → — Company sign-up

  • Employee list

    Search and filter employees, and terminate or reactivate them.

    Learn how → — Employee list

  • Payroll readiness

    See at a glance which employees have setup items blocking payroll, and what each one needs.

    Several payroll requirements cannot be entered yet, so employees currently stay Blocked for live payroll.

    Learn how → — Payroll readiness

  • Time and attendance

    Enter daily worked and overtime hours, then approve or reject them.

    Clock-in/clock-out punches and meal breaks are not yet available.

    Learn how → — Time and attendance

  • Time off

    Submit and review leave requests, see balances, and manage company holidays.

    Time off is managed by an administrator for the employee. Employee self-service, creating leave plans, and manual balance adjustments are not yet available.

    Learn how → — Time off

  • Users, roles, and permissions

    See company members, change their role, remove their access, and read what each role can do.

    Adding people from this screen is not available yet. Roles are fixed.

    Learn how → — Users, roles, and permissions

How it works

  1. Set up your company

    Register your company and Owner account.

  2. Add your people

    Create employees and contractors, keep their documents, and track time and time off.

  3. Check readiness

    See which employees still have setup items to finish before payroll.

  4. Stay in control

    Give teammates the right role and check who did what in Audit History.

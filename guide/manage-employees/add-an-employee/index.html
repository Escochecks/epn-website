Early accessThis guide covers what is available today. Payroll processing is not available yet.

Add an employee

Create the employee

Section titled “Create the employee”
  1. In the side menu choose People → Employees → All Employees, then select Add employee. The Add Employee window opens.
  2. Fill in every field: Employee ID, Role, First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number (with its country code, like +1 555 555 0123), Date of Birth, Hiring Date, Department, Employment Type, Work Location (Default), Pay Type, and Rate. Discipline is optional. The date of birth must be in the past and the hiring date can’t be before it. The rate must be more than zero, with up to two decimals. Pay Type is Hourly, Per Visit, Per Shift, Daily, Salary, or Per Unit/Case, and the label under Rate follows it.
  3. Turn on Is this a new hire? if the person is new to the company. The employee’s profile then shows a New hire badge.
  4. Select Save & Continue. This creates the employee, saves the default work location and the primary pay rate, and opens the employee’s profile so you can finish setup. Press Esc or the close button to leave without saving.

Role is the employee’s job role: what the person does at your company. It is not system access, and choosing one never changes what the person can do in the product. Discipline is a separate field: the worker’s professional classification, such as Registered Nurse. It is chosen from a searchable list, and + Add a discipline adds one of your own. Reimbursement from Medicare or any payer is separate from employee pay.

Add a department

Section titled “Add a department”

If the department you need isn’t in the Department list, choose + Add New Department at the bottom of the list. A small window asks for a name and saves the department for your company, so it is available for every later employee and role. It is then selected in Add Employee, and everything you typed is kept. If the department already exists, it is selected instead of being added twice. The same option is in the Department list of the Create new role window. Adding a department needs permission to manage employees; if you don’t have it the window says so and nothing is added.

Add a work location

Section titled “Add a work location”

If the location you need isn’t in the Work Location (Default) list, choose + Add New Work Location at the bottom of the list. A window opens with the same two tabs you see under Company Details: Branch Details (name, type, address, and whether the location is active for payroll) and Payroll Tax Registration (the registration status and account details you have on file). Fill in what you know and select Add Branch. The location is saved for your company, so it is available for every later employee and under Company Details, and it is selected in Add Employee with everything you typed kept.

  • The window asks for the same required details as Company Details. Account numbers you enter are stored securely and shown only as their last four characters.
  • A new location starts as Active for Payroll so it can be chosen right away. If you turn that off, the location is saved but not selected, because an inactive location can’t be assigned to an employee.
  • Saving a location with registration still incomplete is allowed; the Payroll Tax Registration tab says registration is required before the location can process payroll. Adding a location doesn’t register your company with any tax agency.
  • Cancel or Esc closes the window. If you have typed anything, you are asked first whether to discard it.
  • Adding a work location needs permission to manage work locations. If you don’t have it the window says so and nothing is added; ask a company administrator, or choose an existing location.

The same option is in the employee’s profile, under Work Location: in the Primary Work Location list, and with a + Add New Work Location button under Allowed Secondary Locations. The new location is selected or checked there, and it is applied to the employee only when you select Save Work Location.

Choose a role

Section titled “Choose a role”

Role is a search box. Start typing and the list narrows to your company’s active roles, each shown with its department. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click, to choose one. Press Esc to close the list without closing the window. If you have already chosen a Department, only roles in that department (and roles with no department) are listed, and changing the department clears a role that no longer fits.

  • Roles that are drafts or archived are never listed. Manage them under People → Roles. See Job roles.
  • Choosing a role can suggest a Department, an Employment Type, and a Pay Type, but only in a field you have not filled in yet, and only when the role’s wording matches one of the choices in this window (a role that says “Salary and commission” suggests no pay type, for example). It never changes a value you entered, and it never fills in the Rate.
  • If the role has an employer pay band, the window shows it beneath Rate. See Employer pay band beside Rate.
  • If the role you need isn’t there, choose Create new role at the bottom of the list. A small window asks for a role title and a department, then adds an active role and selects it. Everything you typed in Add Employee is kept. Creating a role needs the right permission; if you don’t have it the window says so and nothing is added.
  • If the company has no active roles yet, the list says so and still offers Create new role.

Employer pay band beside Rate

Section titled “Employer pay band beside Rate”

After you choose a role, the text beneath Rate can show information about that role. It is a guide only: Rate is never filled in for you, and what you type is what is saved.

  • Employer pay band for this role appears when your company has set a band on the role. Anyone who can add employees and view roles sees it.
  • Market median, with its source and year, appears only if you have access to market benchmarks and benchmark figures are available for the role. It is reference data, not a recommendation. Figures used for testing are labeled SYNTHETIC TEST DATA. Nothing appears when benchmarks are unavailable or you don’t have access.
  • If the rate you type is above or below the band, a note says it is outside the employer pay band and shows the band’s limit. When the band and the Pay Type use different units, the rate is converted using the band’s own annual hours and the note shows the converted amount. The note doesn’t stop you: you can still save the employee. It appears only for the Hourly and Salary pay types, and the usual checks on the rate are unchanged.

The note is shown on screen only. It isn’t saved with the pay rate, and a band or benchmark never decides anyone’s pay. For how bands and benchmarks work, see Job roles.

Invite the employee instead

Section titled “Invite the employee instead”

Select Invite Employee instead of Save & Continue to let the employee fill in their own details. It creates the employee, saves the default work location and the primary pay rate, then emails them an invitation. Phone Number and Date of Birth are optional when you invite; every other field is still required. The window confirms that the invitation was sent. If the invitation can’t be sent, the employee is kept and Retry invitation sends it again without creating a second employee.

Each missing or invalid field shows a message under it. If the Employee ID is already used in your company, the message appears under that field and nothing is created. If the employee is created but the work location or pay rate can’t be saved, the window says so, keeps the employee, and offers Retry (which never creates a second employee) or Continue to profile so you can finish there.

The employee profile

Section titled “The employee profile”

After the employee exists, the profile has five steps:

Step What you can do today
1. Employee Setup Edit First Name, Last Name, and Email Address under Personal Information, then select Save Personal Information. Under Work Details, see the employee’s current Job role and open View role assignment history.
2. Taxes & Exemptions Not available yet. This step is planned for a future release.
3. Upload Documents Upload and manage documents. See Employee documents.
4. Additional Settings Add and end benefits and deductions, and set up the employee’s leave plans.
5. Setup Summary See what still blocks this employee from payroll. See Check payroll readiness.

Some fields aren’t available yet

In step 1, the bank name, routing number, and account number fields under Pay Method and Bank Details can’t be entered yet. Only Check is available as a pay method. The Social Security number is shown masked and can’t be changed yet.

Pay Information

Section titled “Pay Information”

In step 1, open Pay Information to see which of your company’s pay schedules the employee is on. You need permission to edit employees to change it.

  • Choose the Pay Schedule from the schedules your company has set up. The Pay Period Start Date and Pay Day are shown for reference and can’t be edited here. They come from the pay schedule itself.
  • The first time, the schedule starts on the employee’s hiring date unless you enter an Effective Date.
  • To move the employee to a different schedule later, select Use a different pay schedule for this employee, pick the schedule, and enter an Effective Date that is after the date of the last change. The earlier assignment stays on record.
  • Select Save Pay Information. If the schedule can’t be checked right now, nothing is saved and you can try again.

Save buttons on the profile

On an existing profile, Save Personal Information is what saves your changes. The footer Save and Exit and Save and Continue buttons only move you around. They do not save.

Benefits and deductions

Section titled “Benefits and deductions”

Open the employee, then 4. Additional Settings. Benefits / Deductions is the first tab. The table lists what the employee has now: Type, Title, Time Period, Amount and Action.

  • Select + Add Benefit / Deduction, choose a Benefit / Deduction Type from the list your company has set up, and enter the Employee Contribution. A Title and a Company Contribution are optional. The Pay Type (a flat amount or a percentage) comes from the type, and the amount is checked to match it. Time Period is not available yet. Save stays unavailable until the form is complete.
  • To use a type that is not in the list, choose + Add Custom Benefit / Deduction. Enter a Title, choose Pre-Tax or Post-Tax and the Pay Type, and select the Tax Withholding Exemptions that apply. The new type is saved for your company and is offered for other employees, but Payroll has to review it before it is used. Until then the row is marked Requires payroll setup.
  • To change one, select Edit on its row, change the values, and select Save. You are asked to confirm, and the change takes effect on the date you choose. The earlier version stays on record. Discard asks you to confirm before it throws away your changes.
  • To remove one, select Delete on its row and confirm. It stops applying going forward. Other employees and earlier payroll are not changed.

Benefit plans are listed separately under Benefit plan elections below the table. Adding, changing and removing these needs permission to manage employee deductions.

Set up a leave plan

Section titled “Set up a leave plan”
  1. Open the employee, then 4. Additional Settings → Employee Leave Plans.
  2. Select + Add Leave Plan. The Leave Plan Setup window opens.
  3. Choose the Leave Plan Type: Vacation Leave, Sick Leave, a custom title your company already uses, or Custom Leave (then enter a Leave Plan Title).
  4. Optionally enter an Effective Date (it is today when left empty) and a Starting Balance (hours). The starting balance is recorded once, as the employee’s opening balance.
  5. Under Accrual Rules, choose an Accrual Method and fill in the fields it shows, then answer the Accrual Period, Maximum balance, Carryover and Negative balance questions. The Save button turns green when everything required is filled in.

The new type from Custom Leave is saved for your company only and is offered for your other employees. It is never visible to another company.

Accrual methods

Section titled “Accrual methods”
  • Yearly - the hours the employee earns each year, with the reset and maximum balance you choose. This is the only method the accrual calculation uses today.
  • Manually Tracking, Every Pay Period and Based on Hours Worked are saved as the plan’s setup only. No hours are added for them yet, so keep tracking those balances by hand. The row is marked Setup only and the window says so.
  • For the last three methods and for Yearly you can add service-duration rules (hours by length of service, with a maximum balance for each). Rules are saved as setup only.
  • A custom reset date, the carryover choices and the negative balance limit are also saved as setup only for now.
  • Check your state and local leave requirements before you choose a policy. This screen records what you choose; it does not decide what the law requires.

Edit and delete

Section titled “Edit and delete”
  • Edit changes only this employee’s settings. The leave plan type, title, effective date and starting balance can’t be changed once saved. Save asks you to confirm and Discard asks you to confirm before it throws away changes. The success message appears only after the change is saved.
  • Delete asks you to confirm, then ends the plan for this employee going forward. The balance history and earlier paystubs are kept, and other employees are not changed.
  • No notification is sent to the employee when a plan changes yet.

Good to know

Section titled “Good to know”
  • The employee is created as Active.
  • Steps 2 to 5 are available once the employee has been saved.
  • Employee ID must be unique within the company.
  • Employee #, Hiring Date, and Status can’t be changed here after creation.

Who can do this

Section titled “Who can do this”

Creating and editing employees needs permission to manage employees, which CompanyAdmin and HRPayrollAdmin have.

  • If the employee can’t be found you will see “Employee not found.” with a link back to People.

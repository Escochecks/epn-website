Employees and people

Keep your workforce records together: who works for you, their documents, their hours and time off, and what each person still needs before payroll.

Your people

  • Employee list

    Search and filter employees, and terminate or reactivate them.

    Learn how → — Employee list

  • Add an employee and edit personal information

    Create an employee and edit their name and email, with leave-plan enrollment.

    Address, tax, bank, and pay details on the profile are not yet available.

    Learn how → — Add an employee and edit personal information

  • Payroll readiness

    See at a glance which employees have setup items blocking payroll, and what each one needs.

    Several payroll requirements cannot be entered yet, so employees currently stay Blocked for live payroll.

    Learn how → — Payroll readiness

  • Employee documents

    Upload, download, and delete documents such as W-4 and I-9 on an employee.

    Learn how → — Employee documents

  • Contractors

    Add contractors and mark them active or inactive.

    Contractor payments are not yet available.

    Learn how → — Contractors

Sensitive identifiers are masked on screen: Social Security numbers show only their last four digits.

Time and time off

  • Time and attendance

    Enter daily worked and overtime hours, then approve or reject them.

    Clock-in/clock-out punches and meal breaks are not yet available.

    Learn how → — Time and attendance

  • Time off

    Submit and review leave requests, see balances, and manage company holidays.

    Time off is managed by an administrator for the employee. Employee self-service, creating leave plans, and manual balance adjustments are not yet available.

    Learn how → — Time off

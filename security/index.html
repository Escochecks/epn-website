Security

Payroll data is sensitive. These are the protections you can see and use in the product today.

  • Company-separated data

    The people, settings, and reports you see belong to the company you have selected. Switching company switches the data.

    Learn how → — Company-separated data

  • Role-based access

    Roles decide what each person can view or change.

    Learn how → — Role-based access

  • Audit History

    Activity in your company is recorded and can be reviewed, with sensitive values masked on screen.

    Learn how → — Audit History

  • Sign-in protection

    Accounts are temporarily locked after repeated incorrect attempts, and changing your password signs out your other devices.

    Learn how → — Sign-in protection

  • Masked sensitive data

    Social Security numbers and EINs show only their last digits where they appear, and bank account numbers are shown only as their last four digits.
